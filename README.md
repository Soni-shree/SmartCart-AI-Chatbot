# SmartCart AI – Intelligent E-Commerce Platform with Conversational AI

SmartCart AI is an AI-powered e-commerce platform inspired by modern quick-commerce applications such as Blinkit and Zepto. The platform combines a responsive shopping experience with an intelligent conversational assistant powered by Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Large Language Model (LLM) concepts.

The system enables users to discover products, receive personalized recommendations, manage their shopping cart, complete purchases, and interact with an AI chatbot for shopping assistance and customer support.

## Executive Summary

Modern e-commerce platforms generate large volumes of customer interactions that require intelligent and scalable support systems. SmartCart AI addresses this challenge by integrating conversational AI into the online shopping workflow.

The platform enhances customer engagement through natural language interactions while simplifying product discovery and purchase decisions. By combining modern frontend technologies with AI-driven user assistance, SmartCart AI demonstrates how intelligent systems can improve digital commerce experiences.
